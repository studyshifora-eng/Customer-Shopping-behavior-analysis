# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview
Analyzed 3,900 customer purchase records to identify shopping patterns, customer segments, product preferences, and subscription behavior to support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, data cleaning, and exploratory data analysis
- **SQL (PostgreSQL):** Business queries and customer behavior analysis
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization

## Key Analysis
- Compared revenue by gender and customer age group.
- Analyzed subscriber vs. non-subscriber spending.
- Identified top-rated and best-selling products.
- Examined discount usage and shipping preferences.
- Segmented customers into New, Returning, and Loyal groups.

## Key Insights
- Analyzed purchasing patterns across **3,900 transactions**.
- Identified product categories and customer segments for targeted marketing.
- Evaluated subscription performance and repeat purchasing behavior.
- Developed recommendations for customer retention, loyalty programs, and discount strategies.

## Business Recommendations
- Encourage subscriptions through exclusive benefits.
- Reward repeat customers with loyalty programs.
- Optimize discount strategies and product promotions.
- Use targeted marketing to reach valuable customer segments.

## Project Objective
Demonstrate practical skills in data cleaning, SQL analysis, exploratory data analysis, dashboard development, and translating data into actionable business insights.
